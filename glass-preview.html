Spendly — Aurora

The palette now shipping in the app, taken from src/constants/theme.ts: indigo ground, violet / azure / fuchsia mesh, every surface glass. No green anywhere — money direction is azure in, rose out, which is colourblind-safe in a way green and red never were.

This is what is in the app now. The colours, glass depths and corner radii on this page are copied from the real tokens, so what you see here is what builds.

  • Switch to Light glass. That is the harder theme for this material — check the stat tiles and the grey “Owes you · Sat, 18 Oct” line still read comfortably for you.
  • Money is azure and rose now, not green and red. Worth sitting with for a minute: it is the one change that alters what a number means at a glance.
  • Two things this page cannot show. The app uses layered translucency rather than true backdrop blur, because React Native has no backdrop-filter — over a smooth mesh the difference is very small. And the Spendly logo is still a PNG with a teal arrow, which needs a new asset from you.
